SQL / POSTGRESQL · BYU STRATEGY 412

Airline route & merger analysis

Analyzed US Department of Transportation data across 10,000+ domestic routes to evaluate the competitive implications of the proposed JetBlue–Spirit merger.

Used window functions to calculate carrier shares across 188 overlapping markets, modeled pre- and post-merger HHI, and flagged routes exceeding the concentration thresholds used in the analysis.

PYTHON / PANDAS / CENSUS API · BYU STRATEGY 412

Grocery store expansion pipeline

Built a geospatial data pipeline to identify promising expansion ZIP codes for Sprouts Farmers Market.

Collected locations for Sprouts, Whole Foods, and Trader Joe’s; enriched ZIP codes with Census income and population data; and combined demographics with competitor density to score underserved markets for Tableau analysis.

R / ECONOMETRICS · BYU STRATEGY 402

Airbnb pricing & demand modeling

Analyzed listings across Austin, Chicago, Washington, DC, Los Angeles, and Minneapolis to explore how price relates to demand.

Cleaned city-specific pricing outliers, estimated demand with ordinary least squares, and calculated price elasticity at median prices. The Los Angeles estimate was −1.05, informing the pricing recommendations.
