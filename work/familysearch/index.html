Helping new users take the first meaningful step.

The problem

New visitors were arriving, but many weren’t reaching the core product experience. Adobe Analytics showed that 59% of 820K monthly visitors added zero ancestors.

My contribution

I mapped the end-to-end user journey to understand where new users lost momentum, then articulated the problem, the rationale for change, and the opportunity to help more people start their trees. I authored PRDs to translate that opportunity into clear requirements. One intervention routed confirmation-email traffic directly into the guided tree builder rather than the main site page.

The outcome

Name-adds among confirmation-email clickers increased from 21% to 35%—a 14 percentage-point lift, or 67% relative increase. Separately, an A/B test simplifying the guided tree builder increased names added by 7%.

Product perspective

The opportunity wasn’t simply to attract more visitors. It was to help existing visitors reach a meaningful action with less friction.
