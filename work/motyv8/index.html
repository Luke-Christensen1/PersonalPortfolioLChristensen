The problem

MOTYV8 serves student athletes building habits and mindset. In discovery with coaches and athletes, simplicity and accountability emerged as recurring themes.

My contribution

I used those themes to guide a journal feature and redesigned core user flows in Figma, reducing the steps needed to reach key content.

The outcome

The journal feature was adopted across six programs serving more than 160 athletes. The redesigned flows cut clicks to core content by 60% and lifted task completion by 10%.

Product perspective

A useful habit-building experience has to fit the user’s routine. Simplicity and accountability shaped both what to build and how people would reach it.
